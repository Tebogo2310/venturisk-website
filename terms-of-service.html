3. Nature of the Service

Decision-support only. Venturisk provides automated screening and risk flagging to assist credit officers in reviewing loan applications. It does not make credit decisions. Every application verdict produced by the Platform is a recommendation — the final lending decision rests entirely with the Client.

3.1 What Venturisk does

  • Analyses submitted bank statement PDFs for signs of tampering or fraud;
  • Reconciles stated transaction totals against the transaction detail in the PDF;
  • Verifies company registration status against CIPC records;
  • Verifies applicant identity information using authorised verification services;
  • Flags VAT registration inconsistencies relative to declared turnover;
  • Produces a structured assessment verdict (Approve / Review / Reject) with a confidence score;
  • Provides a structured override workflow for credit officers to record and justify decisions that differ from the system recommendation.

3.2 What Venturisk does not do

  • Make lending decisions or direct Clients to approve or reject any application;
  • Access live bank account data — Venturisk analyses only the PDF document submitted;
  • Guarantee the accuracy or completeness of third-party data sources (CIPC, AVS);
  • Provide legal, financial, or compliance advice;
  • Act as a credit bureau registered under the National Credit Act 34 of 2005;
  • Store applicant SA Identity Numbers — these are used for verification only and are never written to Venturisk's database.

6. Pilot Stage Limitations

During the pilot stage, the Platform operates with the following known limitations, which Clients are required to acknowledge in writing at onboarding:

  • Gate 3 (Entity Verification): Uses a simulated CIPC dataset, not live CIPC registry data. Live integration is planned for commercial release;
  • Gate 4 (Identity Verification): Uses a simulated AVS (Account Verification Service) response, not live BankservAfrica data. Live integration is planned for commercial release;
  • Gates 6 and 7 (NCR Compliance and Stress Test): Run internally for data collection purposes only. Results are excluded from client-facing verdicts during the pilot stage;
  • Gate 8 (Wisdom Capture): The learning engine is inactive until sufficient override data has been accumulated. No AI-generated recommendations are provided until this threshold is reached.

Venturisk will notify Clients in writing when any limitation listed above is resolved.
