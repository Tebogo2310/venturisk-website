Privacy Policy

Venturisk (Pty) Ltd  |  Registration No: 2026/279043/07  |  Last updated: June 2026

Summary in plain language: Venturisk processes loan application data on behalf of lender clients, for the sole purpose of credit fraud screening. We do not sell data. We do not share data with advertisers. All data stays in South Africa. Applicant ID numbers are never stored.

1. Who We Are

Venturisk (Pty) Ltd ("Venturisk", "we", "us") is a South African technology company providing automated credit application screening services to alternative lenders and financial institutions.

Venturisk is registered with the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC), Registration No: 2026/279043/07, and is headquartered in Johannesburg, Gauteng, South Africa.

Tebogo Monanyane is the designated Information Officer of Venturisk (Pty) Ltd, registered with the Information Regulator of South Africa in terms of POPIA.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Venturisk collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when:

  • Lender clients use the Venturisk platform (portal, API) to submit loan applications for screening;
  • Visitors browse the Venturisk website (venturisk.co.za).

This policy applies to all personal information processed by Venturisk in its capacity as an operator under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA"). Lender clients who submit applicant data to Venturisk are the responsible parties under POPIA and are required to obtain applicant consent before submission.

3.2 Lender Client User Data

When lender client staff use the Venturisk portal or API, we process:

  • Name and email address (account registration);
  • Login credentials (passwords are stored in bcrypt-hashed form — we cannot read your password);
  • Application submission records and override decisions (for audit purposes).

3.3 Website Visitors

The Venturisk website (venturisk.co.za) is a static informational site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or analytics tools that collect personal information from website visitors.

4. How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information only for the following purposes:

  • Credit fraud screening: Running loan applications through our forensic assessment pipeline on behalf of lender clients;
  • Entity and identity verification: Checking company registration and identity details against authorised verification services;
  • Platform operation: Authenticating users, maintaining audit logs, and enabling override workflows;
  • Legal compliance: Meeting obligations under POPIA, PAIA, and other applicable South African legislation;
  • Product improvement: Aggregated, anonymised insights from assessment patterns (no individual personal information is used for this purpose).

We do not use personal information for marketing, profiling, advertising, or any purpose not listed above.

5. Lawful Basis for Processing

Venturisk processes applicant personal information as an operator acting on the documented instructions of responsible party lender clients. The lawful basis for processing is:

  • Applicant data: Consent obtained by the lender client from the applicant prior to submission, and the legitimate interest of the lender in assessing the creditworthiness of loan applicants;
  • Lender client user data: Performance of a service contract between Venturisk and the lender client;
  • Legal compliance: Compliance with applicable South African law.

7. Where Data is Stored

All personal information processed by Venturisk is stored and processed exclusively within the Republic of South Africa, on Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure located in the af-south-1 region (Cape Town, South Africa).

Venturisk does not transfer personal information outside South Africa. No cloud services located outside South Africa are used to store or process applicant data.

9. Security Measures

Venturisk implements the following technical and organisational security measures:

  • Encryption at rest: All database records and stored files are encrypted using AWS-managed AES-256 encryption;
  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted to and from the Venturisk platform uses TLS 1.2 or higher (HTTPS only);
  • Access control: API access requires a Client ID and hashed API key; portal access requires individually credentialled accounts;
  • Network security: Server access is restricted by AWS security groups; SSH access is limited to authorised IP addresses;
  • Data minimisation: SA ID numbers are never written to the database;
  • Backups: Automated daily database backups retained for 7 days;
  • Breach response: A documented incident response procedure is maintained, including the 72-hour notification obligation under POPIA Section 22.

10. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject, you have the following rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act:

  • Right to access: Request confirmation of whether Venturisk holds personal information about you, and request a copy of that information;
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate, irrelevant, or incomplete personal information;
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of personal information that is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected;
  • Right to object: Object to the processing of your personal information on reasonable grounds;
  • Right to complain: Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator if you believe your personal information has been processed unlawfully.

To exercise any of these rights, contact the Information Officer at tebogo@venturisk.co.za.
